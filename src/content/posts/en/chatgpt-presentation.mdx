# How to Create a Presentation with ChatGPT in 15 Minutes

Procrastinating on a presentation because you don't know where to start? ChatGPT can structure it for you, write the slide copy, and suggest design ideas. Everything hinges on a good prompt.

This guide shows you how to get a fully-structured presentation in 15 minutes that you can then polish in PowerPoint or Canva. No more staring at a blank slide wondering what to write.

## Step 1: Write Your Foundation Prompt

First, tell ChatGPT exactly what you're presenting. The more specific you are, the better the output.

```
Help me create a presentation for [target audience].

Title: [Presentation Title]
Goal: [Why this presentation matters]
Key Points: [3-5 main ideas to convey]
Duration: [How many minutes for the talk]
Tone: [Formal / casual / educational / persuasive]

Give me a structure of 8-10 slides. For each slide, tell me what to write.
```

**Real example:**

```
Help me create a presentation for marketing department heads.

Title: "How AI Saves Time for Marketers"
Goal: Convince them to adopt AI tools for their teams
Key Points:
- ChatGPT saves 2-3 hours per day on copywriting
- Midjourney speeds up visual creation 5x faster
- These tools amplify creativity, they don't replace it

Duration: 20 minutes
Tone: persuasive

Give me a structure of 8-10 slides. For each slide, tell me what to write.
```

ChatGPT will return something like:

```
Slide 1: Title Slide
"How AI Saves Time for Marketers"
Your name, date

Slide 2: The Problem
- Marketers spend 2-3 hours daily on routine tasks
- Copywriting, social post variations, design briefs
- Time for actual creativity shrinks

Slide 3: The Solution
...
```

Copy this structure into a document.

## Step 2: Expand Each Slide

Don't ask ChatGPT for everything at once. Work slide by slide. It takes a bit longer, but the result is sharper.

Send a new prompt:

```
For slide [number] titled "[Slide Title]":

Write:
1. Main text (2-3 sentences)
2. Three key bullet points
3. Visual ideas (what to draw, what to show)
4. Data or statistics (if relevant)

Remember: this slide should be clear in 10 seconds of reading.
```

**Example:**

```
For slide 3 titled "How Much Time We Save":

Write:
1. Main text (2-3 sentences)
2. Three key bullet points
3. Visual ideas (what to draw)
4. Data or statistics

Remember: this slide should be clear in 10 seconds of reading.
```

Response:

```
Main text:
"AI tools save the team 10-15 hours per week on average. 
This isn't a replacement for creativity—it's a multiplier. 
The human writes a brief, AI generates options, the human chooses the best."

Key points:
- ChatGPT: 2 hours/day on copywriting → 20 minutes
- Midjourney: a designer's day of work → 2 hours
- Result: more time for strategy and actual creativity

Visual ideas:
- Left side: clock (lots of time wasted)
- Right side: clock (little time wasted)
- Arrow between them with ChatGPT and Midjourney logos
- Bright colors: red/green contrast

Data/statistics:
"McKinsey (2024) research: companies that adopted AI 
increased productivity by 40% in year one"
```

Take this text for your slide.

## Step 3: Get Visual Ideas for Each Slide

Not a designer? ChatGPT will tell you what to create.

```
For the slide titled "[Slide Title]" I need a visual.

Context: [What the slide says]

Give me 3 ideas:
1. Simple (can do in PowerPoint in 2 minutes)
2. Nice (needs Canva or Figma)
3. Professional (needs a designer or Midjourney)

For each idea, tell me:
- What to draw (description)
- What colors
- Where to source the image (Unsplash, Midjourney, your own photo, etc.)
```

Example:

```
For the slide titled "The Problem" I need a visual.

Context: marketers waste time on routine tasks, not enough time for creativity.

Give me 3 ideas:
1. Simple (PowerPoint in 2 minutes)
2. Nice (Canva)
3. Professional (Midjourney)

For each one, tell me what to draw, what colors, where to get the image.
```

ChatGPT returns:

```
1. Simple (PowerPoint):
- On the left side of the slide, place an image of a "tired marketer" 
  from Unsplash. Search for "tired marketer office"
- On the right side, list tasks: email, social posts, design briefs, 
  reports—in large red font
- Colors: black text, red imagery, white background

2. Nice (Canva):
- Use the Canva "Problems Slide" template
- Left half: gradient red → orange
- Overlay icons on the gradient: envelope, social network, design, report
- Right half: white background, task description text
- Font: Montserrat (bold) for heading, Open Sans for body

3. Professional (Midjourney):
- Prompt: "Stressed creative professional surrounded by tasks floating 
  in air, vibrant colors, editorial illustration style, modern art, 
  clean composition"
- Colors: orange, blue, white
- Use this image as a slide background with 30% transparency
```

Pick the complexity level that works for you.

## Step 4: Assemble Everything in PowerPoint or Canva

Now you have:
- Presentation structure (from Step 1)
- Slide copy (from Step 2)
- Visual ideas (from Step 3)

Open PowerPoint, Canva, or Google Slides and start building. This takes 5-10 minutes if you follow ChatGPT's text.

**Assembly tips:**

1. **Don't rewrite the copy.** If ChatGPT wrote well—copy as-is. Your job is to format, not rewrite.

2. **One visual per slide.** Don't cram five images onto one slide.

3. **Consistent design.** If using Canva, pick a template and stick with it. Don't mix fonts and styles.

4. **Readable from the back.** Imagine watching this from the last row. Is the font big enough?

## Bonus: Generate Examples and Stories

If you need a case example or anecdote for a slide, use this prompt:

```
For the slide "[Slide Title]" I need an example or case story.

Background: [What happened, who was involved, what was the problem]

Write a short story (100 words) showing how AI solved the problem.

The story should be:
- Concrete (real numbers)
- Simple (like explaining to a fifth grader)
- Engaging (has tension and resolution)
```

This adds authenticity to your presentation.

## What's Next

Once your presentation is done, you can:
- Email it before the meeting
- Share a Canva link (if you built it in Canva)
- Export as PDF for printing
- Upload to Google Drive for collaboration

If you want video or animations later—that's another story. But for structure and copy, you just did a one-hour job in 15 minutes.
